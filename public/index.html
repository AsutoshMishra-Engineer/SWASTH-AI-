Important Health & Doctor Notice

Notice: Swasth AI is an Informational Guide, Not a Doctor

This service provides educational public health advice and symptom awareness. It cannot replace a clinical physical examination, diagnostic blood tests, or prescribed medication from a licensed medical professional.

1
Ask Symptoms Clearly

Describe clearly what you or a family member are experiencing (e.g. fever duration, headache, vomiting, rash, or animal bite) for accurate health guidance.

2
Immediately Consult a Doctor If:

Symptoms worsen, fever exceeds 3 days, there is persistent vomiting, intense pain, breathing difficulty, or confusion. Visit your nearest Community Health Centre (CHC) or Medical Officer immediately.

3
Never Self-Medicate or Skip Treatment

Do not start, stop, or change prescribed antibiotics, insulin, or blood pressure medicines on your own. Always consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist.

4
24/7 Emergency Helplines

• Medical Ambulance: 108
• National Emergency: 112
• Tele-MANAS Mental Health Support: 14416 (Toll-Free)
